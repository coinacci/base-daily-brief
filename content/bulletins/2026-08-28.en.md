In the official Base video Jesse splits the base case cleanly: Base DeFi sits at roughly $5B, built on crypto assets only. Next move: the world's largest asset class — equity — on the same rails. The ladder: $10B, $20B, $50B, $100B and beyond. Xen restated the "place to trade any asset" frame the same day.

Today's signal is not "what shipped" but how much TVL the stack is supposed to carry. Product surface, custody, and onramps have to scale together.

### Bitso × MXNB: pesos onchain, vault on Morpho
**Source:** @base · @jessepollak · @Bitso — Aug 28

Users can deposit Mexican pesos on Bitso and withdraw MXNB directly on Base. Path: fiat MXN, Bitso, MXNB on Base, Juno vault on Morpho. Jesse called it a 1:1 MXN-to-MXNB onramp and said parity onramps are critical for getting everyday people and businesses onchain. Base added support for 20+ local stablecoins.

Tokenized stocks pull global equity onto Base; MXNB pulls local fiat onto the same DeFi engine. That breaks the "Base = USD/USDC chain" shorthand — if local currency arrives 1:1, yield and collateral open to that currency's native user base. LatAm onramp plus Morpho vault is the local analogue of the USDC-on-Blue story.
