## 📢 NEWS

### Cobalt live — Validity and B20
**Source:** @base · @jessepollak · @XenBH · @buildonbase — Sep 30

Base Cobalt, third network upgrade of 2026, on mainnet. Validity Transactions: submit with a validity set; private until criteria are met, then it lands. Composite Policies combine allowlists and blocklists with OR or AND — reuse a KYC allowlist and a sanctions blocklist without duplicating them. Schedule Multiplier Updates time a corporate action like a split in advance; the UI shows a multiplied balance, raw units and transfer behavior stay the same. seizeWithMemo: an authorized admin reassigns a balance as a transfer; from, to, amount, memo sit onchain. Legal: Base does not control persons, apps or assets; issuers control mint, burn, freeze and seizure.

29 September was "coming." 30 September is the ship. Validity is conditional inclusion under the 24/7 thesis — an order does not wait for a session, it waits for a criterion. The three B20 additions pull the native share standard toward issuer operations. Composite is the policy engine for SPXA and the eligible gate. Multiplier shows a split in UI without touching raw balance. seizeWithMemo is the other face of the "1:1 trust" sentence: the issuer can reassign a balance with a memo-logged transfer. The official account wrote the seizure primitive itself.
