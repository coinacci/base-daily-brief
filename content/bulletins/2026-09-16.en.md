### B20, written into the chain
**Source:** @base · @davidtsocy — Sep 16

Base posted a three-layer B20 explainer. Beginner: Base's native token standard, faster, cheaper, safer, built into the chain. Intermediate: full ERC-20 compatibility — wallets, exchanges, indexers, protocols work; execution is a Rust precompile in the node, not Solidity. Expert: ERC-2612 permits stay; B20 adds memos, mint/burn logic, policy gating, granular pause. David: Coinbase tokenized stocks are issued as B20 tokens natively on Base; the chain-level infrastructure that makes them work is Base.

The official technical text under the AGM's "ERC-20 with stock functions." The share token is not a Solidity wrapper; it is a standard that runs in the node — that is where the gas and throughput claim sits. Policy gating and pause put eligible-jurisdiction and issuer control in the precompile, not only in an app. Not a new listing; a public reading of the standard.
