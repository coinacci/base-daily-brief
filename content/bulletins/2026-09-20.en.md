### 4. Validity and Cobalt: if the condition fails, gas does not burn
**Source:** @0xBoneyBoi · @jessepollak · official weekly — Sep 15–19

On 15 September Validity Transactions were flagged for Cobalt, later this month. Friday a chain engineer closed the fail model. If the predicate does not hold, the transaction never enters the block. It does not revert and burn gas; it expires. Jesse called it the overview of why Validity. The weekly left EIP-8130 for later this year: batch, gas abstraction, multiple keys, rotation, portable accounts.

Gas math changes for limit-style flow and agent ticks. A missed condition does not leave junk onchain. Cobalt is "later this month"; 8130 is "this year." The agent wallet (Bankr × Muse) is the workload waiting on the primitive.
