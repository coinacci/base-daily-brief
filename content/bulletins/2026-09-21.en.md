### o1 Community Takeover — the fee address of an abandoned token
**Source:** @stambouli_o1 — Sep 21

Jerry wrote that Community Takeover is live on o1 Launchpad. The familiar case: a token launched on v4, the original dev left, fees still flow, nobody is steering. The community applies to become creator. After approval and onchain confirmation the new address gets creator rights and future creator fees. Eligibility follows contract version — v4 yes, older contracts no.

This is not a governance token and not a DAO vote. It is a change of the address that collects creator fees. The project is not "revived." The stock-paired surface inherits the mechanism — on an eligible paired token, if the original dev disappears, future fees rotate to the new creator. Official `@base` did not repeat it.
