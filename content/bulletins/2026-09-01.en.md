## 📢 NEWS

### cbZEC and cbHYPE are live on Base
**Source:** @base · @CoinbaseMarkets · @jessepollak · @AerodromeFi · @stambouli_o1 — Sep 1

Two new B20 wrapped assets joined the Coinbase line. cbHYPE is 1:1 to HYPE held at Coinbase; cbZEC is 1:1 to ZEC held at Coinbase. Official contracts came from Coinbase Markets. Aerodrome opened pools and said emissions are incoming. o1 put cbHYPE and cbZEC on the launchpad, terminal, and aggregator. Alexander wrote that the new wraps can also be paired with tokenized stocks.

Official contracts: cbHYPE `0xB200000000000000000000451d033a5000cb479e` · cbZEC `0xB2000000000000000000008501b13360000cb2EC`

A cb wrap is Coinbase's way of putting an exchange-held coin into circulation on Base as an ERC-20. Custody stays at Coinbase; the token can be swapped, LP'd, or posted as collateral in Base DeFi. Last week the chain received equity. Today the wrap family grew. Aerodrome and o1 opening pools and pairing on day one means the liquidity surface was waiting. No volume was published today. What to watch is which venue holds the first depth. The fake-token warning is official: only the contracts above.

### $3.4T of stablecoins moved on Base in August
**Source:** @base · Artemis — Sep 1

Base said $3.4 trillion of stablecoins moved across the chain in August. The source is Artemis's per-chain stablecoin transfer table.

This is transfer volume, not TVL. The same dollar is counted many times. Still, the official account closing the month on this number is a claim about what Base is for — a payments and settlement rail, not a meme chain. Last week's $25M/day stock print looks small next to $3.4T, which is the point — stocks are an early secondary market; stablecoins are the chain's daily job. The right read: in August, Base was one of the rails dollars used onchain. Tokenized stocks and the new wraps add assets on top of that rail.

### Build on Base: the share is a primitive, most products are missing
**Source:** @buildonbase · @jessepollak · @XenBH · @davidtsocy — Sep 1

Build on Base published the official builder essay on tokenized stocks. The thesis: equities are now programmable and composable; the design space is open and most of it is unbuilt. The categories are neobrokerages, personal indices, gifting and corporate rewards, yield stripping and credit, memestocks and agent portfolios. Jesse amplified it as "roughly infinite possibility." Xen wrote that Base is backing teams building on tokenized stocks. David Tso repeated the list as still unbuilt. The essay points at Base Batches 004.

Yesterday's Batches reminder was a slogan. Today's essay is the product map under that slogan. Base does not treat the listing as finished work. Last week's Bitwise ATPs, Aerodrome LP, carry vaults, Credifi, and agent trading were early instances of that list. The essay frames them as a start, not enough. The practical read for a builder: Batches 004 wants an application that uses the share, not a generic consumer app.
