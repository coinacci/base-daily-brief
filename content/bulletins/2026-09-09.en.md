### Share tokens on Ledger
**Source:** @base — Sep 9

Base said Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are live on Ledger. Named names: NVDAc, GOOGLc, AAPLc. Trading is 24/7. The eligible-jurisdiction line sat under the same post.

Not a new share — a new distribution surface. Bitget Wallet and KuCoin Web3 took wraps on 1 September; today a hardware wallet opens to share tokens.
