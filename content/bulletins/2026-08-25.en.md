## 📌 DEEP DIVE — Official article (Base)
**Source:** @base — Aug 24 · separate from daily flow

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks live natively on Base on the B20 standard. Underlying shares held with Alpaca (regulated broker/custodian) under ADGM supervision in a bankruptcy-remote structure; the token holder has a direct claim. No whitelist or platform lock-in — standard B20 (ERC-20 extension): hold, transfer, trade.

Three axes for users: Global access — eligible non-US users reach US equity without a brokerage account, local market, or settlement delays. 24/7 — continuous trading via AMMs, including during earnings, macro events, and weekends. DeFi building block — Aave collateral, DEX LP yield, automated strategies; shares that sit idle in a brokerage become programmable here.

What opens for builders: New products and customers — Apple alongside stablecoins in one wallet, borrowing against stock, serving people who want equities not only crypto. Better DeFi raw material — dividends, stock lending (a multi-trillion TradFi business), wider collateral sets via Aave. Compounding — stock enters a DEX pool, then a lending market, then a structured product; first wave sets the rails every name that follows will ride. Onchain counterparts to TradFi businesses — market making, stock lending, margin and credit, indexing, derivatives. Agentic economy — teams like Virtuals and Treasures already wiring agentic trading on always-on, programmable equity.

Technical note: Dividends and splits use an onchain multiplier so balances do not change and DeFi positions do not break. Full venue and contract list at base.org/stocks. The Ecosystem Fund is seeking builders in portfolio-backed credit and new onchain markets.

In 2018, USDC introduced a global, programmable unit of value and transformed the stablecoin market. The same logic now applies to equity. A chain is only as useful as the assets on it; household-name stocks push Base's product surface well past crypto-native limits.
