### Checking is done, the card is on Base
**Source:** [@base](https://x.com/base/status/2110791921589227714) · [base.org/customers/tuyo](https://www.base.org/customers/tuyo) · [@izqui9](https://x.com/izqui9) — 15 October  

**Summary:**  
Base sat with Tuyo CEO Jorge Izquierdo. The concept of checking and savings has run its course. The customer page: spending, earning and investing in one self-custodial app. The card and account run on Base. The card spends USDC in Earn; it keeps earning until it is spent. **Impact:**  
13 October was the stack, 14 October the teaser, today the product paper. The card draws from the Earn balance — checking and savings in one wallet. Yield is variable, the strategy is not named on the page; it may be Morpho or Aave, it was not written. 100,000 is Tuyo-wide, not Base-card users. 175 million acceptance locations is Visa/Mastercard reach, not active use. No stock line. Self-custodial, no FDIC.
