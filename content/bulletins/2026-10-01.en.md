### Cobalt's engineering footnote
**Source:** @0xyoussea · docs.base.org/upgrades/cobalt/overview — Oct 1

Youssef unpacked yesterday's upgrade. Validity: sign once, attach conditions on Base state. Included only when every condition matches; until then pending, no fee. Each must expire within 60 seconds. Conditions never go onchain. Multiplier is ERC-8056; `updateUIMultiplier` shows a split in the UI, raw balance and totalSupply never change. seizeWithMemo moves a blocked holder's balance to a treasury as a transfer; supply is unchanged.

A 60-second cap is not a "leave the order overnight" promise. Conditions never going onchain means Validity is not a public-mempool order.
