## 📢 NEWS

### New wrapped assets tomorrow
**Source:** @base · @stambouli_o1 · @wagmiAlexander — Aug 31

Base announced a wrapped-asset drop for tomorrow and left the names as a guess. The precedent it quoted is cbBTC, cbETH, cbXRP, cbDOGE, cbADA, cbLTC, and cbMEGA. Model is the same: Coinbase custody with free movement through Base DeFi. o1 said the new assets will be pairable on its launchpad as soon as liquidity is provisioned. Aerodrome signaled it is ready for the drop.

A cb wrapped asset is Coinbase's version of putting an exchange-held coin into circulation on Base as an ERC-20. Last week's launch was an equity primitive. Tomorrow's drop extends the crypto wrap line. Once both live on the same chain, venues such as Aerodrome and o1 can pair a stock token against wrapped crypto. No ticker today. The thing to watch is not the brand of the wrap but where the first liquidity sits.

### Base Batches 004 pulled back toward finance
**Source:** @jessepollak · @XenBH — Aug 31

004 was already open; applications close on 9 September. No new program today. Jesse and Xen narrowed the ask: teams building tokenized stocks, RWAs, prediction markets, and perps. The offer is unchanged — $100,000 and a Demo Day in New York.

Batches is Base's early-stage company program. The signal is which vertical now counts as "the work we want." Between August 24 and 29 the chain received native equity, then LP, ATPs, vaults, credit, and agents on the same primitive. Today's copy names tokenized stocks, RWAs, prediction markets, and perps. Listing the share is not enough — Base wants teams that turn it into collateral, a derivative, or a fund package. Deadline is still 9 September. Only the emphasis moved.

### Access line: a wallet and an internet connection
**Source:** @base · @baseapp — Aug 31

Base wrote that a wallet and an internet connection are enough to reach tokenized stocks. Base App applied the same sentence to itself.

The sentence does not ship a new contract feature — it is a distribution claim. Someone in an eligible country can reach the share token through Base App or any wallet without a brokerage account or T+1 settlement. It is the weekday version of Sunday's "NYSE closed, Base open." The limit sits in the same place — U.S. residents cannot use the product. "A wallet is enough" means eligible jurisdiction plus a wallet, not permissionless global access.

## 🤖 AGENTS & x402

### x402 throughput on Base looks faster
**Source:** @AIonBase_ — Aug 31

According to AIonBase, x402 transactions on Base went from about 230,000 a day in early August to about 1.3 million by month-end — roughly 5.5 times. That print is not from official Base or Token Terminal.

x402 is the protocol that lets an agent pay for a service in USDC over HTTP 402. Last week's official recap said about 90 percent of those agent payments sit on Base. AIonBase's count points the same way. Treat the unit with care — this is a community account's transaction count; bots, retries, and cheap pings land in the same number. The trend is real but the sentence "the agent economy is scaling" waits for Token Terminal or an official Base dashboard. It does not belong today.
