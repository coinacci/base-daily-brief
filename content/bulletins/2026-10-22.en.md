### Stock collateral, USDC borrow
**Source:** [@base](https://x.com/base/status/2113327331607745006) · [base.org/customers/aave](https://www.base.org/customers/aave) · [@StaniKulechov](https://x.com/StaniKulechov) · [@brian_armstrong](https://x.com/brian_armstrong/status/2113389071435256151) — 22 October  

**Summary:**  
Base sat with Aave founder Stani Kulechov. Aave is bringing lending and borrowing to tokenized stocks, on Base. The customer page: Equities Hub, Aave V4, September 2026. An eligible investor outside the U.S. borrows USDC against Coinbase tokenized stocks, without selling a single share. 7 tokenized U.S. stocks as collateral. 24/7 access; collateral prices follow Chainlink’s 24/5 equity feeds. $1 trillion+ in loans originated on Aave since 2020, all versions and networks, not Base only. Aave is independent. Stani: Base has a strong DeFi community and a lot of assets. That helps bootstrap onchain products, an important criterion when choosing a chain. Partnering with Base is the right approach to create global markets for stock-backed lending. Usability, speed and a strong DeFi community led Aave to Base. Brian: a good case study for how crypto is updating financial services, increasingly on Base.

**Impact:**  
25 September’s door was a borrow: post the stock, draw USDC. 13 October the Aave DAO approved incentives. 21 October the incentive opened for USDC suppliers. Today is the customer paper and Stani’s sentence. The 7 names may be part of the 19 non-U.S. list from 9 October; the page does not print them. $1 trillion is Aave-wide. Collateral prices are 24/5; on 21 October’s earnings day cash closed and the pool stayed open, but the price feed is 24/5. Liquidation needs Aero depth; Aave will review limits as supply and depth grow. Eligible jurisdiction still stands.
