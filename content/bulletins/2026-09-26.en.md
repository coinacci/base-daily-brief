### If it is not a wrapper, what is it
**Source:** @davidtsocy — Sep 26

David split two buckets. First bucket: most tokenized stocks are third-party wrappers. The holder has a claim on a debt note, a derivative or a certificate issued by an SPV. Second bucket: Coinbase Tokenized Stocks. Issuer Coinbase. True beneficial equity ownership. The share sits in trust. Create and redeem are in-kind.

Friday Aave V4 took seven tickers as collateral. Morpho had taken the same paper a week earlier. Both protocols assume an answer to "what is this." David wrote the assumption on Saturday. It sits in the same family as the B20 native dividend and split explainer and the "1:1 trust" video. The split matters because DeFi composability also works on a wrapper — accepting collateral does not make the paper a share. In-kind create and redeem is a different door from synthetic netting. Eligible jurisdiction cuts that door. "True beneficial" is a team claim, not a court or counsel print.
