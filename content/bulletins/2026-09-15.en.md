## 📢 NEWS

### Validity Transactions in Cobalt
**Source:** @jonroethke · @jessepollak — Sep 15

Base DevRel said Validity Transactions roll out in the Cobalt upgrade, estimated end of month. EIP-2930 or EIP-1559 transactions execute only when specific onchain conditions are met — conditional flows. Jesse called them a new primitive coming to Base.

Yesterday's EIP-8130 makes the account native AA. Validity txs put a condition on the transaction itself — the chain says "run if this state holds," instead of a keeper or wrapper polling. Natural surface for limit-style flow, launch scripts, agent ticks, collateral ratios. What happens on a failed condition is not clean in the official post. The date is "end of month, estimated"; Cobalt is not locked to a calendar day.

### Occupy: trenches, a stock treasury, an AI Senate
**Source:** @virtuals_io · @jessepollak — Sep 15

Virtuals said Occupy is live on Base. Pick a stock, create a token on it. Every trade buys the stock into a treasury. Tokens on the same stock share that treasury. Ten AI Senators run it — hold, distribute to holders, buy back tokens. Holders pick who sits. Jesse wrote "new virtuals trenches now with stocks? based."

The product form of the 1 September "memes & agents" line. Beside o1 pairing, Virtuals: a meme token fills a share-token treasury. The official Base account did not repeat Occupy today; the signal is Jesse's. Eligible jurisdiction follows the treasury — a contract that buys AAPLc and NVDAc still cuts a U.S. wallet. "AI Senate = BlackRock" is a slogan; the governance model is an experiment, not AUM.
