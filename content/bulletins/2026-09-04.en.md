### Armstrong: as close as you can get to a share
**Source:** @base — Sep 4

Base posted a clip from Armstrong at the AGM. Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are as close as you can get to actually owning a share. If Coinbase went bust tomorrow, you would still own the shares. "We built the right product that sets us up for success for years from now."

Not a listing — the short form of the issuer thesis. The token is a 1:1 claim on a custodied share, not a synthetic price. Armstrong's insolvency sentence moves counterparty risk off Coinbase and onto the underlying custody: even if the wrapper dies, the share is said to remain under the Alpaca and ADGM frame. Eligible jurisdiction still applies.

### Base credit: $1.7B → $2.3B outstanding in 12 months
**Source:** @HorinekPM · @XenBH — Sep 4

Base incentives lead Paige Horinek published a Dune dashboard of every floating-rate credit market on Base. Last 12 months: outstanding loans from about $1.7B to $2.3B (+31%), utilization from 58% to 75%. Morpho supplied from $1.0B to $2.1B. Aave added $200M of deposits in two months. USDC borrowing doubled to $2.0B, 90% utilized. cbXRP collateral went from zero to $45M borrowed against $130M posted.

Wednesday's $500M Coinbase Lend is one row of this table. Loans growing, capital running at higher utilization, USDC borrow demand outrunning supply. Wrapped assets starting to work as collateral shows the 1 September cbZEC and cbHYPE drop can extend into credit.
