### NVDAc printed before the cash open
**Source:** @XenBH · @etheraider — Sep 3

NVIDIA told the SEC on 3 September that it signed a definitive agreement on 2 September to buy Hugging Face. The price is about $11.9 billion in cash plus up to $1 billion in employee equity; close is expected in the first half of 2027, subject to regulators. Aerodrome contributor Etheraider wrote that the headline landed about an hour before traditional markets opened and that the NVDA pool on Aerodrome moved immediately. Xen amplified the same example.

This is the first noisy instance of the 24/7 tokenized-stocks thesis: when the underlying company makes news, the onchain pool does not have to wait for the NYSE open.
