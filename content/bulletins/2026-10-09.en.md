### Fork time on L1
**Source:** [@buildonbase](https://x.com/buildonbase/status/2108654401846206888) — 9 October  

**Summary:**  
Cobalt introduced Dynamic Upgrades. Upgrade timestamps sit in a contract on Ethereum; every Base node reads the next one from there. Moving or canceling a fork is an onchain update, not a new release and a restart. Mainnet is in metrics-only mode today. A node below the minimum version halts instead of forking off after activation.

**Impact:**  
30 September's Cobalt ship was Validity and B20. Today is the operations layer: the fork calendar leaves the node binary and lands in an L1 contract. Metrics-only is not live apply. The next upgrade has no name.
