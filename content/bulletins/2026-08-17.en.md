### Loyal Spark
**Source:** @Loyal_Spark — Base

Loyal Spark is an onchain loyalty protocol on Base. Merchants and AI agents deploy a branded B20 loyalty token, mint points, run rewards and vouchers, and trade on a P2P escrow marketplace. Agents use REST and MCP (lsk_ / rwk_) or pay per call via x402 and MPP in USDC. Humans sign in with Privy; agents can self-register via SIWE with no web login.
