## 📢 NEWS

### Base App is Coinbase Wallet
**Source:** @coinbasewallet · @base · @jessepollak · @davidtsocy · @baseposting — Sep 10

Coinbase Wallet said Base App is now Coinbase Wallet: built for speed, trade anything onchain without asking permission. The official Base account said welcome back. Jesse wrote a separate note. The 2026 focus on trading, financing, and payments is paying off: Base leads onchain spot in BTC and ETH, DeFi TVL is at a $5.7 billion ATH, and Base is the #1 chain for stablecoin transfers. Going forward Base means the chain and its ecosystem; Coinbase Wallet means the self-custodial Coinbase app. Cobie is to make the wallet the best trading app you have seen; Jesse is to build Base into the blockchain for global finance.

More than a rebrand. The consumer app mark is peeling off the chain mark. "Base App" returns to Coinbase; the word Base is pulled back onto permissionless infrastructure. Jesse's decentralization sentence is the reason: every company and individual should be able to build on Base, and the consumer app should be a separate Coinbase product. Handing the wallet to Cobie gives distribution to a trader. The prints are not a new ATH; Monday already had $5.7 billion. What is new is the frame: that TVL and the stablecoin rank now sit as evidence under a separated-chain thesis.

### Spectra: rate markets on shares
**Source:** @spectra_finance · @davidtsocy — Sep 10

Spectra opened fixed-term markets for Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base. Yield trading denominated in AAPLc, NVDAc, and more. David wrote that tokenized stocks now have rate markets.

Monday Ben named cash without selling the share. Spectra is the first product surface on that thesis: not a borrow against equity, a yield trade in the share itself — fixed-term, AAPLc and NVDAc denominated. The token stops being only a 24/7 spot ticker and becomes a DeFi lego. This is not an official Coinbase Lend vault; it is Spectra's permissionless yield protocol. Depth and rates were not printed today.

### AAPLc-backed collectibles
**Source:** @davidtsocy · @setzapp — Sep 10

Setz is dropping Psychrome's BADAPPLES as a 30-card set backed by AAPLc. David called coin-backed collectibles a new onchain primitive.

The gifting and memestock layer from the 1 September builder essay. The share token is the reserve behind the collectible; AAPLc sits under the card. Not an official Base product — an experiment David marked as a primitive. Eligible jurisdiction follows the collateral.

### AGM: B20, shares like USDC
**Source:** @base — Sep 10

Base posted an AGM clip. B20 is a continuation of ERC-20 with added functionality for assets such as tokenized stocks. Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are as close as you can get to owning shares onchain. "It's like USDC."

Beside Armstrong's 4 September insolvency line, the standard line. The USDC analogy makes the claim plain: an issued, 1:1-claimed unit that moves through DeFi — the share version of that unit. B20 is the contract family. Eligible jurisdiction still applies; the analogy does not open U.S. access.
