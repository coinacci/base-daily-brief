### Yield and borrow, for the business
**Source:** [@base](https://x.com/base/status/2110054191419027932) · [base.org/earn-borrow](https://www.base.org/earn-borrow) · [@jessepollak](https://x.com/jessepollak/status/2110098523949547928) · [@XenBH](https://x.com/XenBH/status/2110073212197634523) — 13 October  

**Summary:**  
Base: Earn & Borrow. An end-to-end stack to offer customers stablecoin yield and loans against their crypto. The page: a platform uses Base infrastructure and third-party lending to offer eligible customers variable stablecoin yield and loans against digital assets. A customer deposits USDC, borrowers’ interest becomes variable yield, withdrawal depends on liquidity. The platform chooses the wallet (CDP or any). The vault is optional; a curator picks the Morpho or Aave market and the risk limits. Deposits, loans, repayments and liquidations are onchain. Average USD stablecoin yield, before incentives, 4.45%. Base has commercial relationships with Morpho and Aave. Base and Coinbase do not manage a vault. Jesse: any business can now add earn and borrow on Base. Xen: one financing offering, two products, the stack Coinbase scaled to billions.

**Impact:**  
28 September was Citi’s acceptance, 9 October was ONED USD, 12 October was Grow Credit. Today is the stack, not a product. Coinbase’s own Earn, opened to a bank or a fintech. The stock line is not on the page: collateral is “digital assets,” yield is a stable. 4.45% is an average before incentives, a different bucket from 28 September’s OUSD or the Morpho carry. Liquidation, oracle and depeg risk are the page’s own footnote. “Billions of dollars” is Xen’s scale for Coinbase Earn, not this page’s AUM.
