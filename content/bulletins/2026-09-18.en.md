### B20: stocks are not static
**Source:** @base — Sep 18

Base: stocks are not static. They pay dividends, split, and carry ownership rules. Most token standards were not designed for those changes. B20 handles all of it natively. That is why Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are built on B20.

Wednesday was precompiles and gas. Today is corporate action: dividend, split, rule. The standard face of Armstrong's "dividends integrated, voting soon." Pause and policy gating are how an issuer runs those actions onchain.

### Validity: fail and expire
**Source:** @0xBoneyBoi · @jessepollak — Sep 18
