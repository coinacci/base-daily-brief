## 🔧 DEV

### EIP-8130: one AA standard
**Source:** @jessepollak · @_chunter — Aug 21

**Summary:**
Base engineering argued EIP-8130 is built so chain, wallet, app, and user needs do not conflict: portability, passkeys, session keys, sponsored transactions. The alternative (8141) was framed as forcing a features-vs-performance tradeoff. Jesse's line: "make the EVM work for all chains."

**Impact:**
If passkeys, session keys, and sponsored txs sit on one standard, wallet and app teams stop rewriting AA logic per chain. Base Account UX becomes portable across EVM networks, and L1 enshrinement does not lock the ecosystem into a single-chain design.
